# Café X: a café invitation, printed large

Replacement direction: expressive Mexican café poster lettering, appetizing full-width photography, concha-pink and marigold menu panels, clay red ink, cream paper, and warm cacao. Fraunces is a soft, characterful display serif; DM Sans keeps practical details clear.

The homepage opens with Café X's own still life as an immersive photographic poster. A small invitation to visit and real offerings ground the scale. Below it, a colorful menu sampler, a concha feature, coffee provenance, real gathering photos, and practical visiting details vary the rhythm. Navigation stays short and visible. No theme control, decorative agency mark, or previous site's tagline.

## Surface and interaction
Homepage mode: Persuade. Help visitors picture their next cafecito, then discover the menu or order. One short introductory photo reveal; photo hover and tactile buttons; no continuous marquee, scroll hijack, or repeated reveal blocks. Motion respects reduced-motion. Responsive at 320px upward. Contrast, keyboard navigation, menu search, gallery navigation, and actual relative URLs must work.
