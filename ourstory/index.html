Our Story

FROM MEX
TO SAC.

Mexican roots. A Sacramento home. Coffee that connects the two.

IT STARTS
WITH OUR ROOTS.

CAFÉX brings Mexican coffee culture to downtown Sacramento. Our coffee comes from Nayarit, where generations of growers care for the land and the beans that make each cup possible.

Our founder’s roots in Zacatecas shape what we do: a place to gather, share something good, and feel at home.

The nopal in our identity stands for resilience and growth. The X is where Mexican heritage and the energy of a modern café meet.

TRADITION IN EVERY CUP.
ORGULLO IN EVERYTHING.
